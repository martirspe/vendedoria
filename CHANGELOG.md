# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### UI refinements
- Selecta uses a compact desktop filter row, a single source of section spacing, a branded closing catalog card and an aligned checkout assurance block. Footer identity remains visible during checkout and at every breakpoint.

### Added
- Impulso (`stride`), an editable fashion and footwear storefront template: bold editorial hero, four-product selection, real catalog categories, available-product campaign, collection panels, merchant-supplied testimonials and factual purchase reassurance. Includes responsive styling throughout the existing product/cart/checkout/receipt journey, a recoverable featured-loading error, and console selection for the `moda` industry. A bounded product read shows real remaining units only when five or fewer are available. Discounts, payment methods and delivery copy use store/catalog settings; no fabricated scarcity, reviews or countdowns.
- Catalog facts and bounded AI retrieval: physical, digital and service forms include use cases, exclusions, compatibility, returns and digital license/access conditions, with type-specific completeness. Tenant-scoped PostgreSQL GIN search covers the full catalog and loads 12 candidates instead of a catalog window; the model receives up to 3 bounded product records and bounded history, with token usage in the trace. Public search is paginated, and Selecta resolves products beyond its 48-item featured collection. Digital fulfillment is snapshotted on order lines and delivered only after payment, preserving purchased access across product edits/deletion. Services request coordination without promising a booked slot. Local 10,000-product query-plan evidence and limitations: `docs/catalogo-ia.md`.
- Platform legal documents for VendedorIA (owner MARRSO S.A.C.), prerendered in the console app: Centro legal (`/legal`), Términos y Condiciones, Planes, Pagos y Reembolsos, Política de Privacidad, Política de Cookies, Acuerdo de Tratamiento de Datos (VendedorIA as processor of each business's buyer data) and Política de Uso Aceptable, with summary, numbered sections, table of contents and print to PDF. Owner data and the ReclamoFácil complaints book link live in `features/legal/legal-identity.ts`. The marketing footer links Términos, Privacidad, Cookies and Centro legal next to a "Libro de Reclamaciones" button and ends with "© year VendedorIA · Hecho en Perú"; the owner, RUC and address appear only inside the legal documents. The sign-in screens, the plan payment and Ayuda link to them too. Store footers (Classic and Selecta) no longer show the business name, RUC and address either, only "© year store name"; buyers find the seller identity in the store's Centro legal and terms. Signup and team invites now require an unticked consent checkbox (`acceptTerms`, rejected with 400 when missing) and store the accepted version and date on the user (`User.termsVersion` / `termsAcceptedAt`, migration `user_terms_acceptance`, additive; version in `auth/legal-terms.ts`)
- Payment link as a WhatsApp button. When the sales agent creates an order (flow B), the reply goes out as an interactive `cta_url` message: the order summary (with a bold "Resumen de tu pedido" heading and total) and the business confirmation message above a "Pagar pedido" button, with "Pedido <code>" in the footer, instead of a raw URL in the text. The URL never appears in the reply (the agent is told not to write it and it is removed if it does); Instagram, replies over Meta's 1 024-character limit or a rejected interactive message fall back to the text with the URL at the end. Mensajes shows the "Pagar pedido" button under the agent message and the playground previews it. Product photos now carry a caption with the name in bold, the price ("Desde" when variants cost differently) and "Disponible" or "Últimas N unidades" instead of the internal stock label
- Handoff alerts. When the sales agent hands a WhatsApp or Instagram conversation to a person, every member of the business team gets an email ("Un cliente quiere hablar con una persona") with a button to that conversation in Mensajes and whether the agent was paused. It is sent once per handoff (when the conversation becomes unattended), never includes the buyer's name, phone or messages, is not awaited by the channel webhook and follows `EMAIL_MODE` (nothing is sent in preview)
- Stores can be published without RUC. In Tienda web → Datos legales the merchant picks "Tengo RUC" (business name, RUC and fiscal address, as before) or "Aún no tengo RUC" (`Storefront.sellerType = INDIVIDUAL`): full name, DNI, a notification address and "district, province". The DNI and the notification address are never sent to the store or emails (`storefront/seller-identity.ts`); buyers see the name and the district in the footer, legal pages and order emails. The terms say the seller is a natural person without RUC and does not issue electronic receipts, and the privacy policy drops receipts and SUNAT. The publish checklist validates the identity by seller type. Existing stores stay as "Tengo RUC" (migration `storefront_seller_type`, additive)
- Mi perfil (`/app/profile`, `GET/PATCH /account/me`, `POST /account/password`, `POST /account/sessions/revoke-others`): every console user can see their name, email, role, business and member-since date, edit their name, change their password (current password required, rate limited to 5 per minute, with a strength guide in the form) and close the session on their other devices. Changing the password keeps this device signed in and ends every other business session; access tokens already issued expire within 15 minutes. The email is read-only. The sidebar footer now shows the user's avatar with initials, name and email linking to the profile, and "Mi perfil" is in the Ctrl+K palette. Logging out now also revokes the device's refresh token on the API (`POST /auth/logout`, rate limited) instead of only clearing the browser
- Services in the catalog (first stage, no schedule). A catalog item can be a "Servicio" (`Product.kind`, with an optional approximate duration and where it is done: "En el local", "A domicilio" or "En línea"). Services have no stock (always unlimited, variants included), are never shipped, cannot be a set or a set piece and are left out of Inventario. In the store they show their duration and place and are booked with "Reservar". A cart with only services skips delivery and shipping and asks for an optional preferred date (`Order.serviceNote`, up to 300 characters), which is shown in the order page, the confirmation email and Pedidos and is never a confirmed booking. Mixed carts still need a delivery, free-shipping coupons do not apply to services-only carts, and services-only orders cannot be marked as shipped: in Pedidos they go from "Marcar agendado" to "Marcar realizado". The sales agent presents services as such (no stock words, no delivery question) and never confirms a day or time; after the payment link it says the schedule is agreed afterwards. Catalog import does not read the item kind yet
- Store editor "Crear mi página con IA" (`POST /store/editor/ai/page`). From a short description of the business, it rewrites the texts of the built-in home sections and adds 2 to 4 distinct library blocks below the product grid, replacing the blocks the merchant had added (with a confirmation when there are any). It is applied to the draft as one undo step, and photos and section visibility are kept. It never writes the how-to-buy steps, which depend on the checkout settings, customer reviews, the gallery or the WhatsApp block. The same writing rules as the other assistants apply (no invented prices, promotions or reviews), and it uses one editor text of the plan
- Store editor "Crear con IA" on image fields (`POST /store/editor/ai/image`). It creates an ambiance photo for the cover, banner or "Imagen con texto" with `OPENAI_IMAGE_MODEL` (default `gpt-image-1-mini`, medium quality, landscape; portrait for the Selecta cover). The prompt forbids products, packaging, text, logos and recognizable faces; the merchant can only steer the scene, mood or colors. The gallery is excluded because it is meant for real photos of products, place or team. The photo is stored like an upload of the business (WebP, S3 or local) and applied as one undo step. Each photo uses one "imagen con IA" of the plan, a monthly cap separate from texts and agent replies, and the route is rate-limited to 5 per minute
- Store editor design options and two new library blocks. "Texto destacado", "Beneficios" and "Opiniones de clientes" take a background ("Sin fondo", "Suave", "Color de tu marca"). "Imagen con texto" can put the photo on the left or the right. Options are a new `choice` field kind in the editor contract, validated against their option list on the server. New blocks: "Llamado a la acción" (message plus a button to the products, brand background by default) and, in Classic, "Preguntas frecuentes", which shows the merchant's custom questions or answers built from the store settings (payments, delivery, pickup, returns). Those default answers now live in the shared `core/store-faq.ts` and are no longer Selecta-only

A collapsible side panel lists the template sections as an accordion with the same fields, a "Texto sugerido" restore, and the Selecta FAQ editor. Changes autosave as a draft that only the merchant sees (preview token) until "Publicar cambios"; "Descartar" returns to the published version, and there is undo/redo. Works for Classic and Selecta; texts are kept when switching templates. Content is stored per section in `Storefront.templateContent` (published) and `templateDraft` (draft), sanitized on the server with per-field length limits and only the tenant's own uploads accepted as images. It replaces the old texts form in Tienda web, which now links to "Editar diseño". The store only allows being framed by the console origin (`STORE_EDITOR_ORIGIN`) and the console CSP allows the store subdomains in `frame-src`
- Store editor sections: the merchant can reorder the home sections (drag the handle or use the up/down arrows), hide the optional ones without losing their texts, and add sections from a per-template library ("Imagen con texto", "Texto destacado"; up to 8, repeatable and removable, with undo). The order is stored per template in `templateContent.layouts`; the default order is not saved, so stores without changes follow future template updates, and the server rejects unknown, duplicated or non-hideable entries and drops the texts of removed sections. The footer stays fixed and only its texts are editable

Stored in `templateContent.theme`; a missing option keeps the template look (Classic follows the store colors and logo, Selecta its own palette and monogram). Logo and colors leave the Tienda web form, which now points to the editor, and the logo checklist step counts a logo chosen in the editor. The panel now opens the library and new sections in view

Opening the product section shows one of the store products in the preview, and any home section brings the preview back to the home. Empty editable texts show a hint only inside the editor

Every publish keeps the replaced design (the first one is the template before any edit) in the new `StorefrontVersion` table; an API sweep publishes due drafts every minute and claims the row so a manual and a scheduled publish never apply twice
- Store editor "Escribir con IA" (`POST /store/editor/ai/text`): every text field of the editor gets a writing assistant that proposes up to three texts ("Escribir", "Más corto", "Más vendedor", "Más cercano") or corrects spelling ("Corregir"), with an optional "qué quieres destacar" hint; the merchant picks one and it lands in the draft as one undo step. It only uses the store name, tagline, industry, published product names and categories and the other texts of the section; it never adds prices, discounts or amounts the merchant did not write (the server drops them) and only accepts text fields of the store template. It is a separate writing runtime, not the sales agent; each request uses one editor text of the plan, stops when the month is used, is rate limited, and the button is hidden when OpenAI is not configured

Grants, role changes, revocations, password resets and platform logins are recorded in `PlatformAuditLog`. Migration `0017_platform_operators`
